# Project Sunrise — kick-off notes

> A second Aurora café on the Korenmarkt, Ghent. Target opening: **1 May 2027**.
> All names and figures in this example are fictional.

## Decisions

1. Go ahead with the **Korenmarkt** unit (160 m², terrace for 24).
2. Same menu as Vrijdagmarkt, plus **pour-over** and a small kitchen.
3. The back room gets a visible roaster and doubles as a tasting room for wholesale clients.
